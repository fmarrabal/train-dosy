Software provenance and licensing

nmrglue 0.11 is retained unmodified in vendor_runtime. Its copyright and full
BSD license are vendor_runtime/nmrglue-0.11.dist-info/LICENSE.txt; package
metadata and RECORD are also retained. Copyright (c) 2010-2015 Jonathan J.
Helmus. The pipeline loads the Varian and Bruker modules directly to avoid
an unrelated Tecmag importer incompatibility with NumPy 2.5. This does not
alter the imported modules or the ACME objective source.

The SCORE/LOCODOSY translation is inherited from ../vendor/locodosy_native.py,
with its GPL-3.0-or-later attribution, ../vendor/COPYING-GNAT and
../vendor/ATTRIBUTION.txt. It implements published methods and is not a
novel method of this manuscript. No additional GNAT source code was copied
here; the acquisition-parameter conversion is documented with source hashes
and exact line references in the preprocessing JSON.

The public DOSY raw data were downloaded without authentication from the
Manchester NMR Methodology Group's official DOSY Toolbox archive. The dataset
is attributed separately in public_mixture_references.tex. No explicit data
license was found inside the legacy data archive; do not transfer the CC-BY
license of the unrelated GNAT Mendeley dataset to this older example. The
public download URLs and hashes suffice for reproducible retrieval. Preserve
source attribution and upstream notices when packaging software.

The manual PDF is an attribution/context source; it need not be redistributed
with the reproduction package. The excluded 8-acquisition GNAT example is
documented in GNAT8_INELIGIBLE.json and contributes no fitted result.
