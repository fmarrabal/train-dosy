Compact public reproduction scope

This public subset contains the downloader, preprocessing and fitting scripts,
recorded scalar summaries, attribution and licensed software dependencies.
It does not contain measured raw or processed spectral arrays, fitted arrays,
the source manual, or industrial observations.

From this folder, run download_varian.py first, then prepare_varian.py,
run_varian.py and summarize_varian.py. The downloader checks the official archive
hash and extracts the four required PropPentSuc_DOSY.fid files. Generated
input.npz, results and figures then permit full numerical replay and audit.
See docs/VALIDATION_20261017_EN.md at the repository root for environment pins,
the source dependency and the distinction between prediction and chemical truth.
